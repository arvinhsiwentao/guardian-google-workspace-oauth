Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 29, 2026

Guardian Google Workspace OAuth is a private personal automation used only by Arvin Tao. It accesses Google account data only after explicit OAuth authorization and only for the workflows the account owner requests.

Data used

The automation may read calendar, email, Drive, Docs, or related Google Workspace data needed for calendar-aware health reports, training schedules, and other owner-directed workflows.

Storage and sharing

OAuth credentials and workflow data are stored in the account owner's controlled environment. Data is not sold, used for advertising, or shared with unrelated third parties.

Retention and deletion

Data is retained only as needed for the owner's workflows. The owner may revoke Google access or delete stored data at any time.
